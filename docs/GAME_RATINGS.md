# MotionPlay game ratings (initial estimates)

Scores are **1–5** for **playable** (camera tracking + rules feel fair on a phone) and **engaging** (kids want another round). These are desk estimates from reading the game design and shared plumbing — not playtest scores. Revisit after kid sessions.

Scale: 1 = broken/frustrating · 3 = solid · 5 = delightfully clear and sticky.

| Game | Playable | Engaging | One-line rationale |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Jump the Wall | 4 | 5 | Classic side-scroller cadence; jump/duck reads instantly and ramps well. |
| Balloon Pop | 5 | 5 | Path-based swats forgive camera lag; colourful, obvious, endlessly poppable. |
| Lane Runner | 4 | 4 | Lean-to-lane is clear; hysteresis helps, but lean thresholds need space to move. |
| Fruit Slice | 4 | 5 | Silhouette fruit + real swipe speed feels juicy; bombs keep attention high. |
| Goalkeeper | 4 | 4 | Growing ball sell timing well; reach games are phone-friendly upper-body fare. |
| Punch Out | 4 | 5 | Pad lighting + punch-drive (forward or across) matches how kids actually jab. |
| Sky Flap | 3 | 3 | Hand-height control is clever but easy to overshoot gaps on a small screen. |
| Ski Slalom | 4 | 4 | Continuous lean carving feels good; gates teach precision without lane snaps. |
| Squat Rush | 5 | 3 | Rep detection is fair and forgiving; pure squat banks score but less variety. |
| Pose Match | 3 | 4 | Silhouette goals are charming; hold tolerance can frustrate in odd lighting. |
| Hand Snake | 4 | 5 | Leading a banking snake is novel and readable; tail collisions create drama. |
| Hand Tetris | 3 | 4 | Slide + raise-to-turn is ambitious; gesture disambiguation can feel fiddly. |
| Freeze Frame | 4 | 5 | Green/red statue twist is perfect for kids; adaptive stillness threshold is smart. |
| Body Drums | 3 | 5 | On-beat full-body hits are thrilling when tracking holds; timing is demanding. |
| Orbit Keeper | 3 | 5 | Physics toys with limbs-as-bats feel magical; chaotic when multiple balls fly. |

## Snapshot

- **Top engaging:** Balloon Pop, Fruit Slice, Punch Out, Hand Snake, Freeze Frame, Body Drums, Orbit Keeper, Jump the Wall (5).
- **Top playable:** Balloon Pop, Squat Rush (5); most reach/lean games sit at 4.
- **Needs the most care:** Hand Tetris (gesture clarity), Sky Flap (altitude overshoot), Pose Match / Body Drums / Orbit Keeper (tracking sensitivity under load).
