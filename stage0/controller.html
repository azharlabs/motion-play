Open an external game tab, click the MotionPlay extension icon on that tab to arm it, then start motion control.

Runner profile

Lean left/right for lanes, real jump for jump, crouch for slide. Intended for Subway Surfers-style and endless-runner games.

Racer profile

Lean to steer. MotionPlay holds acceleration automatically while you are visible. Works well with arrow-key racing games such as MIT-licensed javascript-racer.
